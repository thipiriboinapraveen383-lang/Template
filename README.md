## ✨ Features

### 🎨 **Design & Visual**
- **Bold Dark Theme** – Premium dark color palette with neon blue, purple, and cyan accents
- **Glassmorphism UI** – Frosted glass cards with blur effects and glowing borders
- **3D Background** – Dynamic Three.js particle system with floating geometric shapes
- **Custom 404 Page** – Styled error page ready for deployment
- **Retina Ready** – Optimized graphics for high-DPI screens

### 🧩 **Interactive Elements**
- **3D Floating Shapes** – Rotating spheres, torus knots, and particle systems
- **Mouse Glow Effect** – Light that follows cursor movement
- **Project Slider** – Touch-friendly, responsive carousel for projects
- **Media Lightbox** – Click to enlarge project images
- **GSAP Animations** – Smooth scroll-triggered animations
- **3D Card Transforms** – Tilt effect on hover for glass cards
- **Download Resume** – One-click resume download button (template included)

### 📱 **Responsive & Functional**
- **Mobile-First Navigation** – Collapsible hamburger menu on smaller screens
- **Contact Form** – Beautiful form with validation and animations
- **Skill Bars** – Animated progress bars for technical skills
- **Timeline Layout** – Clean work experience presentation
- **Certifications Grid** – Display your credentials professionally
